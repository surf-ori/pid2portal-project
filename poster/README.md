# ORI Quality Monitor poster

Poster for the [Barcelona Declaration conference](https://barcelona-declaration.org/conference_2026_berlin_programme/), Berlin, 24–25 November 2026: *"Better metadata, better decisions"*: how the Dutch Barcelona Declaration signatories monitor and improve their open research information with the ORI dashboard. A0 portrait (841 × 1189 mm), in the SURF Design System, with compositions from the SURF presentation template (cover blocks, purple statement slide, process chevrons, coloured tiles, closing panel). It focuses on the ORI dashboard and on the value of better metadata for the signatories.

- `index.html`: the poster. Online at https://surf-ori.github.io/pid2portal-project/poster/
- `ori-monitor-poster-A0.pdf`: print-ready export of the current version.
- `qr-ori-dashboard.svg`: QR code to https://ori-dashboard.surf.nl/
- `qr-pid2portal.svg` / `.png`: QR code to https://surf-ori.github.io/pid2portal-project/

The dashboard images in `../assets/img/dashboard/`: `live-*.jpg` are captured from the live dashboard (ori-dashboard.surf.nl, November 2026, one participating institution, at 3× resolution); `accuracy-mockup.jpg` is a view from the dashboard mockup (ori-dashboard.lovable.app, as published in the metrics report, doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23682960) with illustrative data, because the live feed does not publish accuracy yet. The organisation list on the poster is the list of participating organisations on the dashboard.

## Editing

- Change the text in `index.html` (every block is marked with a comment), or open the poster in a browser and click **Edit text** to type on it directly. Edits in the browser are not saved: export them with **Save as PDF**, or copy them into the file.
- The four dashboard screenshots grow or shrink to fill the space that is left, so the poster stays one page when you add or remove text.

## Export to PDF

Open the poster in Chrome or Edge, click **Save as PDF** and choose: destination *Save as PDF*, paper size *A0* (or custom 841 × 1189 mm), margins *None*, *Background graphics* on.
