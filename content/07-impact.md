## What changed, and what it makes possible

### Open research information monitoring

icon: research-14
tags: Objective 1

#### Outcomes

- Dutch universities (Barcelona signatories) have a tangible way to set goals on which aspects of open research information to improve, and to track the progress of their interventions.
- New skills and expertise to set up a digitally sovereign data lake tech stack.
- 2 TB of open research information (snapshots from OpenAIRE, OpenAlex and CRIS systems) can be queried in plain language.

#### Potential impact

- Open research information affiliated with a university becomes more complete and of higher quality, in open indexes and in institutional research information systems.
- The open data lake tech stack can be a model for institutional data orchestration and FAIR research data management.
- Non-technical users can query complex data lake structures transparently.

### Netherlands Research Portal

icon: collaboration-10
tags: Objective 2

#### Outcomes

- UKB directors agreed to support infrastructures like the Netherlands Research Portal financially, and to become OpenAIRE members for the sustainability of cornerstone open science infrastructures.
- Dutch repository managers (WISH) agreed to investigate aligning older metadata exchange standards with European standards.
- OpenAIRE added people as a separate entity in the Graph data model and API.

#### Potential impact

- The Netherlands Research Portal is sustainably available.
- Dutch research output is easily reusable in the Dutch node of the European Open Science Cloud (EOSC-NL).
- Dutch universities influence the European strategic agenda of OpenAIRE.
- Researchers log in, search, link research and find their institutional repository to deposit literature, data and software.
- Policy makers and journalists find researchers who can help explain a scientific or societal challenge.
