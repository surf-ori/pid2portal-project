## Put it to work at your institution

Join the onboarding workshop for Barcelona Declaration signatories and learn how to use the ORI dashboard to set goals and plan interventions. Share the interventions you tried, with what they cost and what they brought, so we build a shared list scored on effort and impact. Try the tools, tell us what you think and help us make Dutch open research information better, together.

- [Register for the onboarding workshop](https://communities.surf.nl/open-research-information/evenement/ori-dashboard-on-boarding-workshop)
- [Give feedback on the dashboard](https://github.com/surf-ori/ori-dashboard/issues/new/choose)
- [Join the SURF ORI community](https://communities.surf.nl/en/open-research-information)
