## Put it to work at your institution

Want to use the ORI dashboard at your institution to set goals and plan interventions? Send us an e-mail and we will arrange an onboarding for your team. Share the interventions you tried, with what they cost and what they brought, so we build a shared list scored on effort and impact. Try the tools, tell us what you think and help us make Dutch open research information better, together.

- [Share an intervention](#share-intervention)
- [Request an onboarding](mailto:maurice.vanderfeesten@surf.nl?subject=ORI%20dashboard%20onboarding)
- [See shared interventions](https://github.com/surf-ori/ori-dashboard/issues?q=label%3Aintervention)
- [Join the SURF ORI community](https://communities.surf.nl/en/open-research-information)
