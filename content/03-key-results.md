## Two results we are proud of

Everything we built comes together in two places that Dutch universities can use right now.

### Open Research Information Monitor

tags: In production

![The ORI data quality dashboard, completeness tab](assets/img/ori-dashboard-completeness.jpg)

**Helps universities improve the quality of how their research is represented in open research information infrastructures.**

The ORI data quality dashboard compares your institution's records in your own CRIS with open sources such as OpenAlex and OpenAIRE. It shows what is missing, what can be enriched from elsewhere and where sources disagree, so you can set goals and track the effect of your improvements.

- [Open the ORI dashboard](https://ori-dashboard.surf.nl/)
- [Read the metrics report](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23682960)

### Netherlands Research Portal

tags: In production

![The Netherlands Research Portal home page](assets/img/nl-research-portal.jpg)

**Helps universities show their research output and projects.**

All Dutch research in one place: publications, datasets and research software from Dutch institutional repositories and CRIS systems, linked to research grants and to the organisations that perform the research. Built on OpenAIRE, the European infrastructure for open scholarly communication.

- [Visit the Netherlands Research Portal](https://netherlands.openaire.eu/)
