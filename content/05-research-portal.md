## Strengthening the Netherlands Research Portal

A national portal is only as good as the organisations, repositories and metadata behind it. We mapped the Dutch research landscape, made its status visible and worked with OpenAIRE on what comes next.

### Netherlands Research Portal

icon: network-04
tags: In production

![Netherlands Research Portal](assets/img/nl-research-portal.jpg)

The Dutch community portal on OpenAIRE. Search, link and deposit: publications, research data and software from Dutch repositories and CRIS systems, connected to projects and funders.

- [Visit the portal](https://netherlands.openaire.eu/)

### Dutch research organisations

icon: collaboration-15
tags: Open data, Open source

One curated list of Dutch research organisations, with their identifiers, so every system talks about the same institutions. Published as open data, with the curation pipeline and a dashboard to explore it.

- [Data](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21419893)
- [Code: curation pipeline](https://github.com/surf-ori/nl-research-organisations/)
- [Dashboard](https://surf-ori.github.io/nl-research-organisations/)

### Repositories and CRIS systems

icon: data-10
tags: Live status

Which repositories and CRIS systems belong to these organisations, and are they connected to the portal? A live status dashboard keeps track.

- [View the live status](https://surf-ori.github.io/dashboards/repository-status.html)

### DURF Metadata Monitor

icon: research-05
tags: Dashboard

![DURF dashboards](assets/img/durf-dashboards.jpg)

A research portal compatibility dashboard, built on top of the ORI data lake. It checks the completeness and validity of metadata in Dutch CRIS systems and repositories for publications, persons, organisations and projects.

- [Open the DURF Metadata Monitor](https://durf-project.github.io/dashboards/02-metadata-quality.html#openaire)

### People in the OpenAIRE Graph

icon: collaboration-06
tags: OpenAIRE, Since June 2026

Together with OpenAIRE, people became a separate entity in the OpenAIRE Graph data model (v3) and API. This is the foundation for an expert finder: helping policy makers and journalists find researchers with current and past employment at a university.

- [Person entities](https://graph.openaire.eu/docs/graph-production-workflow/person_entity_pipeline)
- [Graph API: persons](https://graph.openaire.eu/docs/apis/graph-api/persons)
- [Data model v3](https://graph.openaire.eu/docs/data-model/)
