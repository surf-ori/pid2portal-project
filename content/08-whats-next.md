## The work continues in DURF and BROCCOLI

PID2Portal was designed as a bridge. Its results now find a home in two Open Science NL infrastructure projects, so they stay maintained and keep improving.

### BROCCOLI takes over the ORI backbone

icon: data-15
tags: Data curation

![BROCCOLI: metadata providers, enrichment, curation and storage](assets/img/broccoli.jpg)

The ORI dashboard and the ORI DuckLake are absorbed into the BROCCOLI project for data curation and maintenance.

### DURF continues the portal work

icon: collaboration-05
tags: Governance

![DURF: Dutch Repository Federation](assets/img/durf.jpg)

The DURF project continues to coordinate governance, financial sustainability and technical alignment for the Netherlands Research Portal.

- **Governance:** VU initially chairs the national consortium (rolling chair), with rules of participation on the agenda.
- **Financial:** SURF and UKB are working on a service agreement to share the costs of technical and functional maintenance.
- **Technical:** KB, WISH and the Pure user groups align on technical standards.

- [View the DURF roadmap](https://durf-project.github.io/durf-gantt/)

### A national OpenAIRE consortium

icon: collaboration-10
tags: OpenAIRE

More and more Dutch university libraries (UKB) become members of OpenAIRE, and together they will form a National Membership Consortium (Nameco).

### Still on the roadmap

icon: research-19
tags: Next steps

- **Single sign-on** to the Netherlands Research Portal for all Dutch universities. Technically ready; it needs a formal product owner, a legal framework and a data processing agreement.
- **Expert finder** in the portal's user interface, following the person entities now in the OpenAIRE Graph.
- **Consortium agreement** on governance, finances and technical sustainability, as part of DURF.

- [OpenAIRE Connect roadmap](https://connect.openaire.eu/about/roadmap.html)
