## Open research information you can rely on

PID2Portal (PID to Portal) is a project led by SURF to make progress on the [Open Research Information Agenda](https://www.universiteitenvannederland.nl/files/publications/Open%20Research%20Information%20Agenda%20%28Businesscase%20OKB%29.pdf) of Universities of the Netherlands. It bridges the start of two cornerstone Open Science NL infrastructure projects: **DURF** and **BROCCOLI**.

### Improve open research information monitoring

number: 1
tags: Objective

Develop a monitoring framework and ORI-related measurements that give actionable insights into metadata quality, PID adoption and metadata licensing across institutions.

**For:** Dutch signatories of the Barcelona Declaration on Open Research Information (Research Intelligence product owners).

- [See the results](#monitoring)

### Strengthen the Netherlands Research Portal

number: 2
tags: Objective

Make it easier for Dutch institutions to take part in the Netherlands Research Portal, by improving technical workflows and building a national consortium together with OpenAIRE.

**For:** UKB heads of research support (Repository and CRIS product owners).

- [See the results](#portal)
