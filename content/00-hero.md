# Better metadata,<br>*better decisions*

Better open research information about Dutch research, from persistent identifier to research portal. In twelve months, together with the Dutch signatories of the Barcelona Declaration, we built a data quality dashboard, an open data lake and two reports, and laid the groundwork for better governance and funding of the Netherlands Research Portal.
